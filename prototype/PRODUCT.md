# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Facts below were inferred from the user's brief and the earlier audit in this session. No interview round took place. Items marked (inferred) are unconfirmed.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React 19, TypeScript, motion/react, Phosphor icons. Single-file build for sharing. Chosen by the user's brief: a full-stack web app (Next.js, Supabase, React family) rather than a WordPress plugin; this prototype is the front-end demo only.

## Users

Front-desk officers, department staff (housekeeping, engineering, IT, F&B, security), supervisors, the duty manager and the owner at Azalea Hotels. They work in shifts, often at night, on shared phones, between other tasks.

## Product Purpose

A service-request and maintenance desk that keeps working when the internet, the power or the phone signal does not, and that hands work over cleanly between shifts. A possible OCS product for hotel and hospitality clients; Azalea Hotels is the pilot (inferred).

## Positioning

Offline-first on the hotel's own equipment, quiet by default, and fair to staff: reports show departments, never personal rankings. It is a logbook and notifier, never a life-safety system.

## Operating Context

Radios, paper forms and a booking system already exist. The app sits beside them. Safety situations start with 911 and the radio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Requests with per-item owners and clocks, work orders with cost tracking and escalation from a ticket, cleaning with an automatic ready rule, shift handover, department-level insights, a network simulator, a local safety alert. Pilot property and booking system are undecided. All data in the prototype is synthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

The build brief (azalea-ehelpdesk-build-brief.md) and the audit. No customer testimonials, benchmarks or pricing exist and none are claimed.

## Product Principles

1. Never sit between a person and the phone or radio in an emergency.
2. Quiet by default. Sound is for safety and VIP guests.
3. Departments are measured, people are not ranked.
4. Taps first. No required typing in the main flow.
5. Every claim about connectivity is shown working, not described.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Dynamic text size, high contrast, reduced motion, 44 point targets, keyboard use, haptics with a visual equivalent. English UI; safety keywords cover English, Tagalog and Bikol.
