# Azalea e-Helpdesk: Quick start

Print this page and keep it at the front desk. The full guide is `docs/HELP-GUIDE.md`.

Open your hotel's desk at its **/helpdesk/** address. On a phone, use the tabs at the bottom. On a computer, use the menu on the left.

## 1. Sign in

- **With email:** type your email and password. Tick **Keep me signed in on this device** only on your own device.
- **On a shared phone:** tap your name on **Who's on shift?** and type your four-digit PIN.
- Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** and open the email link.
- Forgot your PIN? Ask a manager to set a new one.
- Too many wrong tries? Wait 15 minutes.
- Changing shift on a shared phone? Tap **Switch person**. A PIN sign-in lasts 12 hours. Weak PINs such as 1111 and 1234 are refused.

## 2. Log a request

1. Tap **New request**.
2. Tap the room number (any 3 or 4 digits; the desk does not check it).
3. Tap what the guest needs. Add a short remark if it helps.
4. Tap **Log request**. The right team is told.

Guest still talking? Tap **Log it later (room only)** (save just the room) and finish it from **Finish logging**.

Finished requests leave the list after about 2 days.

## 3. Finish a job

1. Open the request.
2. Tap **Relay** (pass it to the right team), then **On site** (someone is at the room), then **Done**.
3. Waiting on something? Tap **Hold…** (pause it and say why). The timer stops. Tap **Resume** later.
4. Wrong team? Tap **Transfer…** (move it to another team).
5. Cannot be done? Tap **Can't do**. (Duplicate and Cancel have no button.)
6. Big repair? Tap **Escalate to work order** (turn it into a repair job).

## 4. Safety alert

**Danger? Call 911 or use the radio first. Then log.** The desk does not call anyone for you.

- A word like smoke, fire, gas, flood or "trapped" in a remark shows a red warning.
- Tap **Raise safety alert**. Everyone signed in sees it.
- Tap **I called 911 just now** to record the call.
- A false alarm? Tap **Downgrade to false alarm (logged)**.
- Only the hotel owner or a manager can **Resolve with sign-off**.

## 5. Hand over a shift

1. Open **Handover**.
2. Read the numbers and the promises to guests.
3. Tap **Return to the department queue** for requests left by someone who went off shift.
4. The next person taps **Acknowledge handover**.

## 6. Where to get help

- Ask your **hotel owner or manager**. They can set a new PIN or switch a login on.
- Email did not arrive? Check the spam folder. Then call the **web team**.
- No internet? Keep working. The desk saves your work and sends it when the connection returns. Look for **Offline** at the top.
- Power and battery gone? Open **More, Paper kit** on a phone, or use the printed forms.
